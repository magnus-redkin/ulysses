## Useful Links

### Official Documentation

- **[Hiddify Manager Docs](https://hiddify.com)** — official Hiddify panel documentation. Installation, configuration, protocols, client management. Available in English and Chinese.

- **[Xray Docs](https://xtls.github.io)** — Xray-core documentation (V2Ray fork). Detailed coverage of VLESS, XTLS, Reality. Technically in-depth.

- **[Project V / V2Fly Docs](https://v2fly.org)** — V2Ray and related tools documentation. Protocol descriptions (VMess, VLESS, Trojan, Shadowsocks) and transports.

- **[V2Ray Guide](https://guide.v2fly.org)** — official V2Ray guide. More approachable than the main docs. In English.

---

### Communities and Knowledge Bases

- **[NTC Party](https://ntc.party)** — a massive knowledge base on blocking, protocols, clients, ISPs, and terminology. Community-maintained. Translations available in multiple languages, including Chinese.

- **[Privacy Guides](https://privacyguides.org)** — not just about circumvention, but also digital hygiene, VPNs, and anonymity. A good general resource.

---

### Chinese Resources

- **[Project V 官方文档](https://v2ray.com)** — full V2Ray documentation in Chinese. Also available at [v2fly.org](https://v2fly.org).

- **GitHub repositories with guides** — searching for `科学上网 教程` or `翻墙 教程` yields dozens of up-to-date guides for configuring V2Ray, Clash, Hiddify, and more. Chinese users actively adapt to new DPI methods, so materials are updated frequently.

- **[Clash Wiki / Docs](https://github.com/Dreamacro/clash)** — Clash documentation (a popular client in China). Chinese versions available.

---

### Clients and Tools

- **Hiddify App** — the official Sing-box-based client. Available for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS.
- **v2rayN** — the classic Windows client based on Xray-core.
- **Happ** — a cross-platform Xray-core client (with security caveats — see the clients section).
